GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? We get it.

Here are a few things you might want to know before starting.

Do I need any BSL experience?

Level 1 is intended as a starting point for beginners. For Levels 2 and 3, we’ll confirm the appropriate entry requirements before enrolment.

Where will in-person classes take place?

In-person classes are planned for the Coventry area only. The exact venue will be shared when a course is confirmed.

Can I join online from anywhere in the UK?

Yes. Zoom courses are intended to be available nationwide, subject to the confirmed timetable and places.

Are the courses accredited?

Any awarding-body affiliation, assessment and qualification arrangements have not yet been announced. Please check the confirmed course details before booking.

How much do courses cost and when do they start?

Fees and start dates have not yet been set. You can prepare an enquiry below so you’re ready to contact the team once contact details are published.
